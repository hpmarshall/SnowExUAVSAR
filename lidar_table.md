## Notes and caveats

- **QSI 0.5 m and 3 m** products cover identical flights; each is listed once.
- **ASO SnowEx 2020** (Grand Mesa 2/01-02 + 2/13, East River 2/14-20, Taylor 2/20, Reynolds Creek 2/18-19) products are downloaded locally in `ASO_data/` (git-ignored); their footprints in the KML are exact, read from the product GeoTIFFs. **ASO SnowEx 2017** (Grand Mesa 2/08, 2/16, 2/25) is not in CMR; dates from SnowEx17 literature, footprint approximated from the QSI Grand Mesa granule.
- **USCOUB (Uncompahgre 2014-03-20)** granule in `ASO_50M_SWE` has corrupt CMR spatial metadata (a California polygon); its footprint was instead read from the downloaded product GeoTIFF.
- **Senator Beck / Uncompahgre local archive**: the user located and provided local copies of ASO Senator Beck/Uncompahgre products not available in NSIDC/CMR or the ASO public bucket, archived to `ASO_data/SenatorBeck_local/` (5.3 GB, git-ignored). This confirmed the real SnowEx17 Senator Beck snow-on flight date is **2017-02-16** (not Feb 8, as literature suggested) and added two intensity-only Uncompahgre surveys (2015-04-29, 2016-06-04) with no snow-depth product. Footprints for all of these, plus higher-precision re-derivations of the two `ASO_3M_PCDTM` snow-off DTMs, were computed directly from the local GeoTIFFs (nodata/zero-masked, downsampled for large rasters).
- **Snow-off DTMs**: Uncompahgre (2014-09-10) and Senator Beck (2016-09-26) bare-earth reference surfaces are included for context even though they fall outside the Jan-Apr snow season and outside the lidar-UAVSAR overlap KML (no UAVSAR flight within 3 months of either).
- **SNEX_MCS_Lidar** (Mores Creek Summit, 13 Jan-Apr acquisitions 2022-2025) post-dates the UAVSAR flights but sits on the repo AOI and the lowman lines; flagged "outside 2014-2021".
